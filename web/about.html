About this

This is a free, account-free way to teach a young child to read at home. It is 120 short lessons — 15 to 30 minutes each, done sitting next to a grown-up. A child who does one a day gets through it in about four months; one every other day takes eight. Neither is better.

How it teaches

  • Sounds, not letter names. The letter m is taught as /mmm/, not "em".
  • Blending from the very first week. Knowing the sounds is easy; pushing them together into a word is the hard part, so it starts on day 2 and never stops.
  • Nothing appears before it is taught. Every word in every sentence and story can be sounded out with the letters taught so far — checked by a script, not by eye. The handful of words English spells unreasonably ("the", "said") are taught as whole words, on purpose, on their own day.
  • No losing. There is no timer, no score and no failure. A word the child finds tricky simply comes back at the end of the lesson.

Where the order comes from

The order concepts are introduced follows the scope and sequence that Mentava publishes, which is a well-thought-out ordering of a body of phonics knowledge nobody owns. The order is the only thing borrowed: every word, sentence, story, activity and line of code here was written from scratch, and this site is not affiliated with Mentava.

Privacy

There are no accounts, no logins and no analytics. Which days a child has finished is kept in that browser's own storage and never sent anywhere. The voice is the one built into the browser, so nothing is downloaded either.
